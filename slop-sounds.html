Revealed 2026-09-23

There’s a genre of youtube channel that’s been going around for awhile now that has hacked a music discovery flow I was using and it keeps pissing me off.

Attributes:

  • posts daily or near daily
  • electronic|jazz|dnb ai generated music
  • an ai generated cover/thumbnail, either on-brand or just to hook you
  • titles that paint a pov, such as “how it feels to smoke 10 cigarettes” or “this mix will fix you at 3am”
  • 3min of good intro music followed by 57min of “good enough” background slop noise
  • a vid description like “me and 3 friends work hard to post daily” or something more vague like “follow your dreams here is our work”

Feels like I’m going crazy, the amount of channels that fit this MO that I will comment “ai slop” and insta-block them, but they keep popping up.

A hilarious factor here is that I’m a barberbeats enjoyer! If you don’t know, it’s a genre that’s just rehashed music samples slapped together together, and probably is a comparable pre-slop-era genre.

ｂａｒｂｅｒｂｅａｔｓ The genre is called this because of one of the initial popular artists in the space, Haircuts for Men. As I type this one of their cassettes is sitting on the shelf next to me.
